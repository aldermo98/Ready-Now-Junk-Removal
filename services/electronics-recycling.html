E-Waste and TV Removal in West Sacramento and Sacramento

In California, TVs, monitors, computers and many other electronics can't go in the regular trash. Ready Now Junk Removal picks up old electronics from your home or office and takes them to electronics recyclers, so the metals and components are recovered instead of ending up in a landfill.

  • TVs of any size, including old tube TVs
  • Computers, laptops, monitors and printers
  • Office electronics and server equipment
  • Pickup from inside your home or office

Electronics We Take

  • Flat-screen, projection and CRT TVs
  • Desktop computers and laptops
  • Monitors, keyboards and peripherals
  • Printers, copiers and fax machines
  • Stereos, speakers and game consoles
  • Microwaves and small appliances

Why Electronics Need Special Handling

Electronics contain materials like lead, mercury and other metals that the state treats as universal waste. That's why curbside trash services won't take them. We separate electronics from the rest of your load and deliver them to recyclers. For business cleanouts, we can remove electronics along with desks, chairs and other office furniture. Please wipe or remove hard drives before pickup if they contain sensitive data.

E-Waste Removal FAQs

Do you take old tube (CRT) TVs?

Yes. We take TVs of every type and size, including heavy older tube TVs.

Do you destroy hard drives?

We don't offer certified data destruction. Please remove or wipe drives that contain sensitive information before pickup.

Can you pick up from a business?

Yes. We handle office e-waste and can remove it together with furniture and other items during a commercial cleanout.
