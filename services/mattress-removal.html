Mattress Removal & Disposal in West Sacramento and Sacramento

Old mattresses are bulky, hard to carry, and you can't just leave them on the curb or in a dumpster. Ready Now Junk Removal picks up mattresses, box springs and bed frames from any room in your home and hauls them away. When a mattress can be recycled, we take it to a recycling facility instead of the landfill.

  • Pickup from any room, including upstairs apartments
  • Mattresses, box springs, toppers, futons and bed frames
  • Single-item pickups or add-ons to a bigger load
  • Same-day or next-day service often available

What We Take

  • Twin through California King mattresses
  • Box springs and foundations
  • Memory foam, hybrid and pillow-top mattresses
  • Futons and sleeper sofa mattresses
  • Bed frames, headboards and bunk beds
  • Cribs and toddler beds

Why Not Just Leave It on the Curb?

Leaving a mattress on the street can get you a citation in most cities, and many apartment complexes charge tenants for it. California also runs a statewide mattress recycling program, because mattresses are mostly steel, foam and fabric that can be recovered. We handle the carrying and the trip to the right facility, so you don't have to rent a truck or wrestle a king-size mattress down the stairs.

Mattress Pickup Near You

We pick up mattresses throughout West Sacramento, Sacramento, Davis, Woodland, Elk Grove, Roseville and the rest of the Sacramento region. If you're also replacing the bed frame or other furniture, we can take it all in one trip.

Mattress Removal FAQs

Do you take wet or stained mattresses?

Yes, in most cases. Let us know ahead of time if a mattress is soaked or has bed bugs so we can bag it properly.

Can you take just one mattress?

Yes. Single-item pickups are fine, and adding a box spring or frame usually costs only a little more.

Do you recycle mattresses?

When the mattress is in recyclable condition, we take it to a recycling facility rather than the landfill.
