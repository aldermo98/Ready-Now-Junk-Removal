Shed Removal in West Sacramento and Sacramento

An old shed that's leaning, rotting or rusting out takes up valuable yard space and can become a safety hazard. Ready Now Junk Removal tears down wood, metal and plastic sheds, hauls away all of the debris and anything stored inside, and leaves the spot clean and ready for whatever comes next.

  • Wood, metal, vinyl and plastic sheds
  • Contents cleared and hauled in the same visit
  • Debris loaded, removed and the area raked clean
  • Metal and clean wood recycled when possible

How Shed Removal Works

Send us a few photos showing the shed and how we'd get to it. We'll give you a quote based on size, material and access. On removal day we empty the shed if needed, take it down, load every piece, and clean up nails and small debris. Before we arrive, please disconnect any electrical service to the shed. Concrete slabs and foundations can sometimes be removed for an additional charge, so ask when you book.

Shed Removal FAQs

Do you remove what's inside the shed too?

Yes. We'll haul away the contents along with the structure, and set aside anything you want to keep.

Do I need a permit to remove a shed?

Usually not for a typical backyard shed, but rules vary by city and HOA. Check with yours if the shed is large or has electrical service.

Can you remove a concrete slab?

Sometimes. Slab removal depends on size and thickness, so let us know when you request a quote.
