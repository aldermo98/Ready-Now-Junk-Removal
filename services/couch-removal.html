Couch & Sofa Removal in West Sacramento and Sacramento

Couches are the single most common thing people call us about. They're heavy, they don't bend, and they never seem to fit back through the door they came in. Ready Now Junk Removal carries out sofas, sectionals, loveseats, sleeper sofas and recliners from any room and hauls them away the same day when possible.

  • We do the lifting, even from upstairs and tight hallways
  • Sectionals and sleeper sofas welcome
  • Good-condition couches are donated
  • Upfront price before we start

What We Remove

  • Sofas and loveseats
  • Sectionals (any number of pieces)
  • Sleeper sofas and futons
  • Recliners and lift chairs
  • Ottomans and accent chairs
  • Outdoor and patio couches

Donate, Recycle, or Dispose

If your couch is clean and in good shape, we'll try to get it to a local donation center so it gets a second life. Couches that are worn out, stained or broken go to licensed disposal facilities, and we separate out any metal frames for recycling. Either way, you don't need to arrange a charity pickup or rent a truck.

We remove couches in West Sacramento, Sacramento, Davis, Natomas, Elk Grove, Roseville, Folsom and surrounding cities. Getting new furniture delivered? Book us for the same day and we'll clear the old set out first. Also see furniture removal and mattress removal.

Couch Removal FAQs

Do I need to take the couch outside first?

No. We remove it from wherever it is, including living rooms, bedrooms, bonus rooms and upstairs units.

Will you disassemble a sectional?

Yes. We'll separate sectional pieces and remove legs if needed to get them out without damaging your walls or doorframes.

How fast can you come?

Same-day or next-day pickup is often available, especially in West Sacramento and central Sacramento.
