Locally Owned in West Sacramento

Junk Removal in West Sacramento, Sacramento & Yolo County

Ready Now Junk Removal is a locally owned junk hauling company based in West Sacramento. We remove furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, yard waste and construction debris for homeowners, renters, landlords and businesses across the Sacramento region. You get an upfront price before we start, and we do all the lifting, whether your items are in an upstairs apartment, a packed garage or the back corner of the yard.

Because we're based near the Tower Bridge, West Sacramento, Sacramento, Davis and Woodland are where we can usually offer the fastest same-day pickups. We also serve Elk Grove, Natomas, Roseville, Folsom, Citrus Heights and the rest of the region. Good-condition items get donated, metal and e-waste get recycled, and the rest goes to licensed facilities.

Popular services: couch removal, mattress removal, appliance removal, garage cleanouts, estate & eviction cleanouts, construction debris removal and dump trailer rental.
