**Quick answer:** In the Sacramento area you can get rid of a mattress for free in four ways:

1. **Drop it off at a Bye Bye Mattress recycling site.** California's statewide program takes mattresses and box springs free, and the Yolo County Central Landfill near Woodland is one of the sites.
2. **Book your city's free bulky item pickup.** The City of Sacramento offers up to two a year, West Sacramento two a year, and unincorporated Sacramento County one.
3. **Have the store take your old one when you buy new.** California retailers must offer to take your old mattress at no extra cost.
4. **Give it away,** if it's clean and in good shape.

If you can't wait for an appointment or can't move it yourself, a junk removal company like [Ready Now](/free-quote) can pick it up from any room, usually the same or next day.

Below is how each option works, plus the catches.

## 1. Drop it off free with Bye Bye Mattress

California charges a small recycling fee on every new mattress sold. That money funds **Bye Bye Mattress**, a program run by the Mattress Recycling Council that lets residents drop off mattresses and box springs **for free** at participating facilities. About 75% of a mattress (steel springs, foam, fabric and wood) can be recycled.

- **Find a site:** use the locator at [ByeByeMattress.com](https://byebyemattress.com/find-a-facility/). Participating locations and hours change, so check before you load up.
- **Near West Sacramento and Davis:** the **Yolo County Central Landfill** (between Davis and Woodland) collects mattresses through the free Bye Bye Mattress program.
- **The catch:** you need a truck or van, plus someone to help you lift. A queen mattress weighs 50 to 100+ pounds and doesn't fit in most cars.

## 2. Use your city's free bulky item pickup

Most local garbage services include free bulky pickups that cover mattresses. Rules as of 2026:

| Where you live | Free pickups | How to book | Key rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **City of Sacramento** | Up to 2 household junk pickups per year (February–October) | Call 3-1-1 or use the city's online portal | Pile no bigger than 4' × 4' × 9'; out by 6 a.m., no more than 24 hours early |
| **West Sacramento** | 2 bulky pickups per year (July 1–June 30) | Waste Management, (866) 844-1508 | After both, ask the city about a landfill voucher |
| **Unincorporated Sacramento County** (Carmichael, Fair Oaks, Antelope, Rio Linda, Orangevale, etc.) | 1 per 12 months; extra pickups $25 | SacGreenTeam app or 3-1-1 | Pile up to 8' × 4' × 4' |

**The catch:** you have to carry the mattress to the curb yourself, and you may wait a week or more for an open appointment. Leaving a mattress out without an appointment counts as illegal dumping and can get you fined.

## 3. Let the store take it back when you buy a new one

If you're replacing your mattress, this is the easiest free option. Under California's mattress recycling law, **retailers must offer to take back your old mattress at no extra cost** when they deliver a new one. That includes online purchases, which have up to 30 days to arrange the pickup. Ask about take-back before you check out.

## 4. Give it away (if it's in good shape)

A clean, stain-free mattress that's only a few years old can find a new home through Facebook Marketplace, Nextdoor or a local Buy Nothing group. Most thrift stores and charities **don't** accept used mattresses for hygiene reasons, so call before you drive one over.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't leave it on the curb or in an alley.** Without a scheduled pickup, that's illegal dumping, and cities issue fines.
- **Don't squeeze it into an apartment dumpster.** Most complexes charge tenants for bulky items left behind.
- **Don't hire an unlicensed "cash hauler" off a flyer.** Sacramento County warns that if your items get dumped illegally and traced back to you, you can be held responsible for the cleanup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I drop off a mattress for free near Sacramento?

At a participating Bye Bye Mattress facility. Use the locator at ByeByeMattress.com to find the closest one; the Yolo County Central Landfill near Woodland is one of them. Check hours before you go.

### Does the City of Sacramento pick up mattresses for free?

Yes. Worn-out mattresses can go in the city's free household junk pickup, which residents can book up to twice a year from February through October by calling 3-1-1.

### Can I put a mattress in my garbage bin or dumpster?

No. Mattresses are bulky items and need a scheduled bulky pickup, a recycling drop-off, or a hauler. Leaving one by a dumpster or on the curb without an appointment can be treated as illegal dumping.

### Will Goodwill or the Salvation Army take a used mattress?

Usually not. Most donation centers don't accept used mattresses for hygiene reasons. Call your local store first.
