Quick answer: To get rid of old furniture in Sacramento:

  1. Sell or give it away online if it's in good shape.
  2. Donate it to Habitat for Humanity's ReStore or another charity.
  3. Book a free bulky pickup through your city (City of Sacramento residents call 3-1-1).
  4. Drop it at a transfer station yourself.
  5. Hire a junk removal company to carry it out and haul it away.

Which is best depends on the furniture's condition, how fast you need it gone, and whether you can move it yourself.

1. Sell it or give it away

Good-condition furniture moves fast on Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist and Nextdoor. Solid wood dressers, dining sets and newer sectionals sell best. To get it gone quickly:

  • Price it low or list it as free, curbside pickup. Free listings usually go within a day.
  • Take clear photos in daylight and include measurements.
  • Have the buyer pick up, and keep a friend around for safety.

Local Buy Nothing groups on Facebook are great for giving away furniture to neighbors in West Sac, Natomas, Midtown and other neighborhoods.

2. Donate it

Donating keeps furniture out of the landfill and helps local families.

  • Habitat for Humanity Greater Sacramento ReStore: 819 North 10th Street, Sacramento. Donations are accepted Wednesday–Saturday, 10 a.m.–3 p.m. Call (916) 440-1215 to ask about donation pickup for larger items.
  • The Salvation Army, St. Vincent de Paul and local thrift stores also take furniture in good condition.
  • Call first. Most charities won't take items with stains, tears, pet damage or missing parts, and few accept used mattresses.

3. Use your city's free bulky item pickup

If your furniture isn't worth donating, your garbage service probably includes free bulky pickups:

  • City of Sacramento: up to two free household junk pickups per year, February–October. Book by calling 3-1-1 or online. Keep the pile within 4' × 4' × 9', set it out by 6 a.m., and don't put it out more than 24 hours early. Residents also get a free dump coupon for one carload, mailed with the yearly service guide.
  • West Sacramento: two free bulky pickups per year (July–June) through Waste Management, (866) 844-1508.
  • Unincorporated Sacramento County: one free bulky pickup every 12 months through the SacGreenTeam app or 3-1-1. Extra pickups are $25.

See our full guide to free bulky item pickup in Sacramento.

4. Take it to the dump yourself

If you have a pickup truck or trailer, you can haul furniture to a transfer station or landfill. Expect a disposal fee based on weight or load size, unless you're using a free dump coupon. Bring a helper and straps, and cover your load; driving with an unsecured load can get you a ticket.

5. Hire a junk removal company

This is the easiest option when you don't have a truck, the furniture is heavy or upstairs, or you're on a deadline like a move-out. A good junk removal company will:

  • Carry it out of any room, so nothing has to go to the curb
  • Give you an upfront price before lifting anything
  • Donate pieces that are still usable and recycle what they can

Tips to make furniture removal cheaper

  • Group items together. One pickup for several pieces costs less than separate trips.
  • Stage items in the garage or driveway if you can do it safely. Easier access means less labor.
  • Sell or donate the valuable pieces first, then have the rest hauled.
  • Ask your neighbors whether they have junk too. Some companies, including ours, offer discounts for nearby jobs booked together.
