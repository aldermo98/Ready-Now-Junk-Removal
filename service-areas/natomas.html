Can you work with my HOA's rules?

Is Natomas covered by the City of Sacramento junk pickup?

Natomas is inside the City of Sacramento, so residents with city garbage service can use the city's free household junk pickup through 311. Call us when you need it gone sooner, need items carried out, or have more than the city will take.
