City Junk Pickup vs. Hiring a Junk Removal Company

If you live in the City of Sacramento, the city's free household junk pickup (booked through 311) is worth knowing about. It works well when you can wait for an appointment, carry everything to the curb yourself, and keep the pile within the city's size limits. Hiring us makes more sense when:

  • You need it gone this week, or today
  • Items are heavy or inside the house: upstairs, in a back bedroom, or in the attic
  • You've already used your free pickups for the year, or the pile is too big
  • You're moving out, selling, or turning over a rental on a deadline
  • You'd rather not haul a couch to the curb and leave it overnight

Sacramento Junk Removal FAQs

How much does junk removal cost in Sacramento?

Single items start at $99 (first floor or curbside), a full pickup truck load is $169, and each additional load is $140. Tires, appliances and grills cost extra. Text photos to (916) 249-1009 and we'll give you an upfront price before we start.

Does the City of Sacramento pick up junk for free?

Yes. City of Sacramento residents with city garbage service can book free household junk pickups (and separate appliance/e-waste pickups) through 311, generally February through October, with size limits on the pile. It's a good option if you can wait for an appointment and carry everything to the curb yourself. We're the faster option when you need it gone this week or need it carried out of the house.

Do you offer same-day junk removal in Sacramento?

Often, yes. We're based just across the river in West Sacramento, so Downtown, Midtown, Land Park, Natomas and East Sac are usually quick for us to reach. Call or text early in the day for the best chance at a same-day slot.

What won't you take?

Hazardous waste such as paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks and asbestos. City and county household hazardous waste facilities accept those. We take almost everything else, including furniture, appliances, e-waste, yard waste and construction debris.

Do I have to be home?

Not always. For curbside or garage pickups, many customers pay by card and send us photos ahead of time. For anything inside the home, someone needs to let us in.
