Davis Junk Removal FAQs

Do you help with student move-outs in Davis?

Yes. Move-out season around the end of the UC Davis school year and at lease turnover is our busiest time in Davis. Book a few days ahead if you can. We'll haul couches, mattresses, desks and everything else left behind.

Can you pick up from upstairs apartments?

Yes. We carry items down stairs and out of tight hallways, so you don't have to drag anything to the dumpster yourself.

How far is Davis from your base?

We're in West Sacramento, about 15 minutes east of Davis on I-80. That makes Davis one of our core service areas, and same-day or next-day pickups are often possible.

Do you donate furniture?

When items are in good condition, we donate them instead of dumping them. That's especially common with student furniture that's only a year or two old.
