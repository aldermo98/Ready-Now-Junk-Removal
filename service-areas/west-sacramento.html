West Sacramento Junk Removal FAQs

Do you offer same-day junk removal in West Sacramento?

Often, yes. We're based in West Sacramento, so local jobs are the easiest for us to fit in. Call or text early in the day with photos and we'll tell you the soonest time we can be there.

How much does junk removal cost in West Sacramento?

Single items start at $99 (first floor or curbside), a full pickup truck load is $169, and each additional load is $140. Tires, appliances and grills cost extra. Send photos and we'll give you an upfront price before we start. There are no surprise fees.

Do I need to move things to the curb?

No. We remove items from anywhere on the property, including bedrooms, garages, backyards and upstairs apartments. Our prices assume first-floor or curbside access; stairs or difficult access may add a fee, which we'll explain before we start.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance for my apartment or HOA?

Yes. Many Bridge District and Southport buildings ask for one before move-outs. Let us know when you book and we'll send it to your property manager.

What can't you take?

We can't haul hazardous waste such as paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks or asbestos. Yolo County runs household hazardous waste drop-off for those items. We take almost everything else.
