Junk Removal Pricing FAQs

How much is single-item junk pickup?

$99 for one item, such as a couch, mattress, recliner or dresser, when it's on the first floor or curbside with normal access. Appliances, tires and grills have an additional fee.

How much is a full truck load?

A full pickup truck load is $169, and loads can be heaping. Every additional truck load is $140, so two loads are $309 and three loads are $449.

What costs extra?

Tires ($9 to $67), appliances ($10 to $35) and grills ($10) have item fees that match the Yolo County Central Landfill fee schedule, and hazardous waste is quoted per item. Stairs, long carries, difficult access or hazardous conditions may also add a fee at our team member's discretion. We always explain any extra fee before we start.

Do you charge extra for stairs?

Our prices assume first-floor or curbside access. Carrying items up or down stairs, or long and difficult carries, may add a fee, and we'll tell you before any work starts.

How do I know how many loads I have?

Text 2 to 3 photos of everything to (916) 249-1009 and we'll estimate the number of loads and give you a price. We confirm the final price on site before we lift anything.

Do you offer discounts?

Ask about our Bundle 'N Save discount: when you and your neighbors book pickups together, everyone saves. Current deals are on our promotions page.

Where is the nearest dump to West Sacramento?

The closest public landfill to West Sacramento, Davis and Woodland is the Yolo County Central Landfill at 44090 County Road 28H in Woodland, open Monday to Saturday 6:30am to 4pm and Sunday 8am to 4pm. A full-size pickup load of household trash is $32 there under the July 2026 fee schedule.

How much does it cost to dump a truck load in Sacramento?

Under the 2026 Sacramento County fee schedules, a pickup truck load is $40 at the North Area Recovery Station and $35 at Kiefer Landfill. At the Yolo County Central Landfill it's $32 for a full-size pickup. Uncovered loads cost extra. Private Sacramento transfer stations charge minimums of about $50 to $72 per load.
