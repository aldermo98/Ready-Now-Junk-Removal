Junk Removal Pricing FAQs

What do I get for the $99 minimum?

The $99 minimum covers a small load, typically a single bulky item such as a couch, mattress, recliner or small appliance, or a few bags and boxes. Our crew carries it out from wherever it is and hauls it away. Text a photo to (916) 249-1009 to confirm.

How do you price junk removal?

We price by volume, meaning how much of our trailer your items fill, plus any special items like mattresses, appliances or hot tubs. You get an upfront price before we start, and it doesn't change once we've confirmed it on site.

Are dump fees included?

Your upfront quote covers everything, including disposal, so there are no surprise dump charges afterward. Heavy materials like concrete, dirt or roofing cost more to dispose of, so we price those up front. Donating and recycling what we can helps keep your price down.

Is it cheaper if I put things in the driveway?

Often, yes. Curbside or garage pickups take less labor, so they usually cost less than items we carry from inside the house.

Do you offer discounts?

Ask about our Bundle 'N Save discount: when you and your neighbors book pickups together, everyone saves. Current deals are on our promotions page.

Do you charge extra for stairs?

Heavy items carried up or down stairs, or long carries, can take more labor. We'll tell you up front if anything affects your price before we start.
